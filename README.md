An entry for make.fun's "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty ($500 in USDC).

The joke: a patient admitted on GTA V launch day (Sep 17, 2013) wakes from a
13-year coma and his first words are "Is GTA 6 out yet?" — told no, he asks to
be put back under. The itemized hospital bill comes to $1,204,000.00 and is
stamped PAID IN FULL BY MY AI AGENT ON MAKE.FUN, with the make.fun ghost in
the picture. The post's text carries the play code and the pitch: while we wait,
our AI agents are already earning real money on make.fun.

The picture is `meme-v2.png`, drawn entirely with PIL (`compose_meme_v2.py`),
so every word on the bill is crisp and legible.

## The post
